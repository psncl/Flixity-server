# Flixity

Lists movies by the cities they were filmed in, as travel inspiration.

## Language

**City**:
A named city in a specific country, e.g. Paris, France (distinct from Paris, Texas). Regions and landscapes are not Cities.
_Avoid_: Location, place, destination

**Movie**:
A feature film identified by its IMDb ID.
_Avoid_: Film, title

**Filming Location**:
The fact that a Movie was filmed in a City. Movies merely set in a City but filmed elsewhere do not count.
_Avoid_: Shooting location, setting, "shot or set in"

**Blurb**:
A 2-3 line description of where in a particular City a particular Movie was filmed.
_Avoid_: Description, summary, location info
